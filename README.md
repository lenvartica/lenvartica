### 🚀 About Me

## 👨‍💻 Lenny Muriuki — Deçan  
I’m **Lenny Muriuki**, also known as **Deçan**, a passionate **full-stack developer, technology creator, and aspiring tech entrepreneur from Kenya**. I’m fascinated by software, artificial intelligence, web and mobile development, automation, and the endless possibilities of technology.  
I enjoy turning ideas into real digital products — from websites and mobile applications to APIs, automation systems, AI-powered tools, and online platforms. My development journey includes technologies such as **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, Node.js, Flutter/Dart, React, Firebase, and modern web technologies**.  
My projects include **Deçan Konnect, Deçan Movie Box, Deçan Video Tube, Deçan Music, Deçan Shortener, Thunder XMD**, and other experimental technology projects. I’m constantly learning, building, testing, breaking things, fixing them, and improving my skills.  
Beyond coding, I’m interested in **artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, automation, digital businesses, content creation, and entrepreneurship**. My long-term ambition is to build a successful technology company and become a major player in the global tech industry.  
### 🚀 My Vision
